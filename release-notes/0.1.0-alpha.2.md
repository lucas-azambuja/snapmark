Third alpha: a new capture flow and screen recording.

## What's new

### Capture
- **Nothing is captured until you say so.** After you select, the area stays on screen with handles to resize or move it, and an action bar appears next to it.
- The action bar has two groups: **Video** (system audio, microphone, Record) and **Screenshot** (timer, copy, save, Capture).
- **Window mode** (Space) stays in the overlay: the front window is highlighted right away, hover to pick another one, click to choose it. **Hold Shift** for the whole screen.
- A hint bar at the bottom of the screen shows the available keys.
- **Screenshot timer** (3, 5 or 10 s), and **Copy** / **Save** straight away without opening the editor.
- White corner marks instead of a colored border, and a pointing-hand cursor in window and screen modes.
- Clicks no longer fall through to the app under the overlay.

### Screen recording
- Record an area, a window or a whole screen as MP4 (H.264, up to 60 fps), with the cursor.
- Optional **system audio** and **microphone** (macOS 15+), with live level meters and a microphone picker.
- The screen stays dimmed around the recorded area while recording. A floating control shows the time, the audio meters, **Stop** and **Discard**. ⌘⇧5 or the menu bar icon also stop.
- Capture warms up during the 3-second countdown, so recordings start sharp and with sound.
- **Review window** after recording: filmstrip timeline with trim handles, scrubbing, Space to play, a waveform per audio track with volume and mute, and **Trim and Save**.

## Install
1. Download `Snapmark-0.1.0-alpha.2.zip`, unzip it, and move `Snapmark.app` to `/Applications`.
2. The app isn't signed with an Apple certificate, so macOS blocks the first launch. Try to open it once and click **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security, or run:
   ```bash
   xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Snapmark.app
   ```
3. Allow Screen Recording when Snapmark asks. To record your voice, also allow the microphone.

Requires macOS 13 or later (the loupe needs macOS 14, the microphone needs macOS 15). Universal binary (Apple silicon and Intel).
