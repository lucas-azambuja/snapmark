**First beta of Snapmark 0.1.0.** Everything planned for 0.1.0 is in: capture, annotation, OCR, screen recording, and settings. From here on, the focus is fixing bugs and polishing before the final 0.1.0.

## What's in Snapmark 0.1.0
- **Capture** an area, a window or a whole screen with one shortcut (⌘⇧5 by default, configurable). The selection stays on screen to adjust, with a loupe and **snap to edges**, and it remembers the last area.
- **Action bar** next to the selection: screenshot timer, **Copy Text (OCR)**, copy, save, capture, and **Record**.
- **Annotation editor**: arrows, lines, rectangles, ellipses, pen, highlighter, blur, text and numbered counters. Every annotation stays editable: select, move, resize, recolor, delete.
- **Screen recording** to MP4 with system audio and microphone (macOS 15+), live audio meters, a dimmed background, and a floating Stop control.
- **Review recordings**: filmstrip trimmer, per-track waveforms with volume and mute, then **Trim and Save**.
- **Floating thumbnail** after copying, saving or recording: drag it into any app, or click to reopen.
- **Settings** for the shortcut, save folder, image format, recording frame rate and more, plus a daily **update check** against GitHub releases.

No changes since 0.1.0-alpha.4 other than the version.

## Install
1. Download `Snapmark-0.1.0-beta.1.zip`, unzip it, and move `Snapmark.app` to `/Applications` (replace the old one).
2. The app isn't signed with an Apple certificate, so macOS blocks the first launch. Click **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security, or run:
   ```bash
   xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Snapmark.app
   ```
3. Allow Screen Recording when Snapmark asks (if it already looks enabled, remove it with – and add it again).

Found a bug? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/lucas-azambuja/snapmark/issues).

Requires macOS 13 or later (the loupe and snapping need macOS 14, the microphone needs macOS 15). Universal binary (Apple silicon and Intel).
