Fifth alpha: snapping, settings, and update checks.

## What's new
- **Snap to edges** (macOS 14+). While you draw, resize or move a selection, its sides snap to the visible edges on screen: windows, cards, panels, dividers. An accent guide line shows where it snapped. Press **G** or click *Snap* in the hint bar to turn it on or off; hold **⌥ Option** to skip it for one drag.
- **Settings** (menu → *Settings…*, or ⌘,):
  - Change the **capture shortcut** (Reset goes back to ⌘⇧5).
  - Launch at login, sounds, update checks.
  - Remember last area, snap to edges, crosshair and loupe, window shadow.
  - Save folder and image format (PNG or JPEG).
  - Recording frame rate (30 or 60 fps) and cursor.
- **Remembers the last area.** The next capture starts with the previous selection in place (unless it was a whole screen), ready to capture again with Return.
- **Update check.** Snapmark looks for new releases on GitHub once a day. When there's one, the menu bar icon shows a dot and the menu shows **Update Available**. There's also **Check for Updates…** in the menu.

## Install
1. Download `Snapmark-0.1.0-alpha.4.zip`, unzip it, and move `Snapmark.app` to `/Applications` (replace the old one).
2. The app isn't signed with an Apple certificate, so macOS blocks the first launch. Click **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security, or run:
   ```bash
   xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Snapmark.app
   ```
3. Allow Screen Recording again when Snapmark asks (if it already looks enabled, remove it with – and add it again).

Requires macOS 13 or later (the loupe and snapping need macOS 14, the microphone needs macOS 15). Universal binary (Apple silicon and Intel).
